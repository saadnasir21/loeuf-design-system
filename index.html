01

Brand essence

loeuf — from l'œuf, the egg — is built on the idea of the essential: stripped back, beautifully made, endlessly wearable. The system below keeps every touchpoint calm, confident and intentional.

Quiet, not loud

Generous negative space, restrained palette, one accent at a time. The product is the hero — the interface steps back.

Crafted with intention

Every element earns its place. Considered detail over decoration; precision over noise.

Made to move

Warm neutrals and effortless rhythm that feel as fluid and lived-in as the clothes themselves.

02

Logo & wordmark

The wordmark is set lowercase in Gotham Medium with wide tracking (~0.28em). The accent dot is the brand's smallest signature — the one place the Rose accent may appear in the mark.

loeuf.Primary on Blanc
loeuf.Reversed on Noir
loeuf.On Mist surface
Clear space & size

Keep clear space of at least the cap-height of the "l" on all sides. Minimum digital size: 80px wide. Tracking never drops below 0.2em.

Misuse
Don't recolor the wordmark (gold dot excepted)
Don't condense, outline, or add shadow
Don't set in the serif display face
Don't place on busy imagery without a scrim

Imagery & art direction

Photography is editorial and lived-in: natural light, neutral sets, garments in motion, generous negative space. Mist and Almond fill empty frames and image placeholders. Never over-saturate or over-retouch.

Neutral sets

Room to breathe

Crop generously and leave space — quiet frames feel premium.

08

Voice & tone

loeuf speaks like a thoughtful friend with great taste: warm, understated, and assured. Short sentences. Sensory, never salesy. A light French inflection in product names, plain Pakistani warmth in service.

Do

  • "Crafted for the way you move."
  • Lead with feeling and fabric, then detail.
  • Keep it short, calm and specific.
  • Use italic accent words for rhythm — style, season.
  • Be transparent about shipping, sizing and returns.

Don't

  • Shout — "MEGA SALE!!! BUY NOW!!!"
  • Over-explain or pad with filler adjectives.
  • Chase hype or trend-speak.
  • Use more than one accent color per view.
  • Crowd the layout — let white space speak.
