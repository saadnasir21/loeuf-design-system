01

The œuf motif

The name means the egg — so the egg becomes the system. A soft, slightly-tapered oval is the one shape we own. Use it to crop product shots, frame quotes, build highlight icons and replace the "o" in the wordmark. One shape, infinitely wearable.

product crop
lifestyle
  • Crop — mask hero product & lifestyle shots inside the oval on a Mist field.
  • Frame — outline-only egg holds quotes, prices and CTAs.
  • Mark — the egg replaces the "o" in leuf and dots the highlights.
  • Peek — a half-egg sliding off-frame teases drops & "coming soon."

  • Poll — "this or that" colourways to learn what to restock.

09

Captions, hooks & hashtags

Warm, short, sensory. Lead with feeling, then the detail, then one clear action. Open with a hook — the first line is the whole game.

Caption formula
  • 1Hook — one evocative line.
  • 2Feel — a sensory line about fabric / movement.
  • 3Detail — name, fabric, sizing or price.
  • 4Action — one CTA (link in bio / DM to order).
  • 5Tags — 8–12, grouped by reach.
